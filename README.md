# Comprehensive OSINT Threat Assessment: Left-Wing Extremism (LWE) in India

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project is an open-source intelligence (OSINT) investigation detailing the historical evolution, socio-economic roots, and current geographical contraction of the Naxalite-Maoist insurgency in India[cite: 1]. By leveraging geospatial intelligence (GEOINT), social media intelligence (SOCMINT), and advanced search operators, this report maps the transition of the "Red Corridor" from a widespread internal security threat to a localized insurgency[cite: 1]. 

## 🎯 Key Intelligence Findings
* **Geospatial Contraction:** Documented the shrinking of the insurgency footprint from over 165 affected districts in 2005 to highly concentrated pockets primarily within Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, and Odisha by 2024-2025[cite: 1].
* **Threat Neutralization:** Tracked the elimination of key leadership, including CPI (Maoist) General Secretary Nambala Keshava Rao in May 2025, using real-time social media monitoring and official portal verification[cite: 1].
* **Infrastructure & State Penetration:** Visualized the transformation of isolated conflict zones through GEOINT, demonstrating the correlation between rapid infrastructure development (e.g., the Malkangiri Gurupriya Bridge) and the decline of extremist influence[cite: 1].

## 🛠️ OSINT Methodologies & Tools Applied
* **Advanced Search (Google Dorking):** Extracted official datasets, situation reports, and press releases from government domains (e.g., `site:pib.gov.in`, `filetype:pdf`)[cite: 1].
* **GEOINT (Geospatial Intelligence):** Utilized Google Earth historical imagery to conduct before-and-after topographical analysis of conflict hotspots, mining belts, and critical infrastructure[cite: 1].
* **SOCMINT (Social Media Intelligence):** Applied targeted query filters on X (formerly Twitter) to monitor real-time incident reports, security encounters, and organizational statements[cite: 1].
* **Data Triangulation:** Cross-referenced raw intelligence from the South Asia Terrorism Portal (SATP), the National Investigation Agency (NIA) Most Wanted portal, and ACLED datasets for robust verification[cite: 1].

## 💼 Relevance to Corporate Risk & GRC
While focused on a national security threat, the investigative methodologies demonstrated in this report are directly applicable to Corporate Security and Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC). The ability to conduct non-intrusive footprinting, analyze external threat landscapes, and synthesize raw data into verifiable intelligence is critical for protecting corporate assets, assessing regional operational risks, and ensuring regulatory compliance.
